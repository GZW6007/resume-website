# George Wu Resume Website

A personal resume and portfolio website for George Wu (吳哲誌), focused on marketing data, business analytics, and AI-enabled workflows.

## Website Content

The website presents:

- A professional profile and personal introduction
- Education and academic background
- Professional development certificates (Google 數位人才探索計畫)
- Work experience in marketing data, business intelligence, and revenue operations
- Skills covering data analysis, marketing analytics, research, automation, and generative AI
- A portfolio of selected projects related to competitive intelligence, media monitoring, customer segmentation, pricing analysis, dashboards, and business strategy
- Contact information and links for connecting with George

## Technologies

- HTML5 for the page structure and content
- CSS3 for responsive layout, visual design, animations, and interactive styling
- Vanilla JavaScript for scroll-based header behavior, cursor effects, and project card interactions
- Google Fonts, including Noto Sans TC and Rajdhani
